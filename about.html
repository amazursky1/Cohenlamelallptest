About Cohen & Lamela, LLP

Broad experience. Personal representation.

Cohen & Lamela is a full-service Union, New Jersey law firm serving individuals, families and businesses. The firm combines decades of courtroom and transactional experience with the direct attention of a small local practice.

Our Firm

More than 30 years of representing New Jersey clients.

Cohen & Lamela describes its broad range of experience as one of the firm's strengths. Clients come to the firm for matters involving personal injury, criminal law, municipal court, traffic and license issues, domestic violence, landlord-tenant disputes, real estate and other legal needs.

The attorneys' backgrounds include work on multiple sides of the legal system — from insurance defense to prosecution and public defense. That perspective helps the firm evaluate a matter practically, explain the options clearly and prepare a strategy around the client's specific circumstances.

The firm's goal is straightforward: provide experienced representation with the accessibility and individualized service clients expect from a smaller local practice.

Attorney Profiles

Jeffery M. Cohen

Attorney

Decades of Union County courtroom experience.

Jeffery M. Cohen graduated from Seton Hall Law School in 1988. He began his career with a Bergen County firm before returning to Union County, where his work included representing private clients and defending insurance carriers in negligence matters.

In 1993, Mr. Cohen opened his own practice to devote more time to his growing client base. He was appointed Municipal Prosecutor for the Township of Union in 1998 and served in that role for five years. During that period, he also served as president of the Union County Municipal Prosecutors Association.

Mr. Cohen later became Public Defender for Union Township. His experience includes numerous bench and jury trials in both Superior Court and Municipal Court, including DWI, criminal and personal injury matters.

Enrique M. Lamela

Attorney · Fluent in Spanish

Trial experience with direct bilingual client access.

Enrique M. Lamela's early legal experience included a clerkship with the Honorable Margaret M. Neill of Missouri's 22nd Judicial Circuit and insurance-defense work with the Law Offices of John Wendler in St. Louis.

After law school, Mr. Lamela joined his current partner's practice. His principal areas of work include personal injury, landlord-tenant matters, real estate, criminal law and municipal court representation.

Mr. Lamela served as Public Defender for the Borough of Kenilworth in 2004. He appears regularly in both Superior and Municipal Court and has trial experience in each. His fluency in Spanish allows Spanish-speaking clients to communicate directly with their attorney.
