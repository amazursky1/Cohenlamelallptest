Auto Accident Law

Practical representation after a motor-vehicle accident.

A car accident can quickly become complicated. In addition to vehicle damage, a client may be dealing with injuries, medical appointments, insurance adjusters, lost time from work and questions about who was responsible for the collision. Cohen & Lamela assists clients with legal matters arising from automobile accidents and helps them understand the claims process from the beginning.

The firm reviews how the collision occurred, the available evidence and the insurance issues that may affect the claim. The attorneys can then explain what steps may be appropriate and help clients avoid trying to navigate a serious accident claim entirely on their own.

Accident matters may include

  • Rear-end, head-on and intersection collisions
  • Multi-vehicle accidents
  • Crashes involving distracted, careless or impaired driving allegations
  • Accidents resulting in significant medical treatment or lost time from work
  • Disputes involving insurance coverage, responsibility or the value of an injury claim

How the firm can help

  • Review police reports, photographs, witness information and other available evidence
  • Discuss injury-related and insurance issues arising from the collision
  • Explain the legal process, important deadlines and possible paths forward
  • Communicate with insurers and other parties when appropriate
  • Negotiate or litigate the matter when necessary based on the circumstances

Important information after an accident

Details can become harder to reconstruct as time passes. Keep copies of the police report or incident number, photographs, repair information, medical records, insurance correspondence and any contact information for witnesses. If an insurance company has contacted you, keep a record of those communications as well.

What to expect when you call

The attorney will want to understand where and how the collision happened, whether anyone was injured, what medical treatment has occurred and what communication has already taken place with insurers. That information helps the firm identify the most important legal and factual issues at the outset.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do after a car accident?

Seek appropriate medical attention, preserve photographs and reports, exchange insurance information and keep copies of any communications you receive. If legal questions arise, an attorney can help review the situation.

Should I speak with the insurance company before calling a lawyer?

You may receive calls from insurers soon after an accident. Before making detailed statements or signing documents, it can be helpful to understand what is being requested and how it may affect the claim.

What if the other driver says I caused the accident?

Disagreement over responsibility is common. Police reports, photographs, witness statements, vehicle damage and other evidence may all become relevant when evaluating how the collision occurred.

Can the firm help if I received traffic tickets from the accident?

Traffic summonses and an injury claim can involve separate legal issues. The firm can review the full set of documents and explain which matters may need to be addressed independently.
