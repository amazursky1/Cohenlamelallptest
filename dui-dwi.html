DUI / DWI

Focused guidance for New Jersey DUI and DWI matters.

A DUI or DWI charge can raise immediate questions about driving privileges, court appearances, testing procedures and what may happen next. These matters are generally handled in New Jersey municipal court, where familiarity with local court procedure can be particularly valuable.

Cohen & Lamela represents clients facing DUI and DWI allegations and helps them understand both the court process and the factual circumstances surrounding the stop. The firm draws on substantial municipal-court experience when evaluating the charge and preparing a defense.

A DUI / DWI review may involve

  • The reason given for the initial traffic stop
  • Observations made by the investigating officer
  • Field-sobriety testing and roadside procedures
  • Breath, blood or other testing issues when applicable
  • Video, police reports and other available evidence
  • Related traffic summonses arising from the same incident

How the firm can help

  • Review the circumstances leading to the stop and arrest
  • Examine available reports, testing information and related evidence
  • Explain municipal court procedure and the issues that may affect the case
  • Identify potential factual or legal questions requiring further review
  • Represent the client at municipal court appearances and proceedings

Bring every summons and court notice

DUI and DWI incidents can involve more than one charge. When you contact the firm, have all summonses, complaints, notices and paperwork available, not just the document that references the DUI or DWI allegation. The complete set of paperwork can help the attorney understand the scope of the matter.

What to expect when you call

The initial conversation will focus on the stop, what occurred before and after you were pulled over, what testing was requested or performed and whether a court date has already been scheduled. The attorney can then explain what additional documentation may be important and what the next steps in municipal court may look like.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to a DUI or DWI consultation?

Bring every summons, court notice and document you received during or after the stop. If you have information about testing or related charges, bring that as well.

Will a DUI or DWI case be heard in municipal court?

New Jersey DUI and DWI matters are generally handled in municipal court. The specific procedure can depend on the municipality and the charges involved.

Why does the reason for the traffic stop matter?

The circumstances leading to the stop can be one of several issues an attorney may review when evaluating the case, along with testing procedures, observations and available evidence.

What if I received several tickets during the same stop?

Provide all of them. Related traffic summonses may raise additional issues and should be reviewed together rather than one at a time.
