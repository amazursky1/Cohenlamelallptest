Real Estate Law

Clear legal guidance through a real estate transaction.

Buying or selling real estate involves more than agreeing on a price. Contracts, inspection issues, financing, title matters, closing documents and deadlines can all affect whether a transaction reaches the closing table smoothly. Cohen & Lamela assists clients with real estate matters and helps them understand the legal obligations created throughout the transaction.

The firm's role is to review the legal documents, identify issues that require attention and help clients make informed decisions as the transaction progresses. Having an attorney involved early can be useful when questions arise concerning contract language, inspection concerns, title issues or closing requirements.

Real estate matters may involve

  • Residential purchase and sale transactions
  • Contract review and attorney-review issues
  • Inspection-related negotiations and contract questions
  • Title, closing and document-related concerns
  • Questions involving obligations before closing
  • Legal issues that arise when a transaction becomes disputed or delayed

How the firm can help

  • Review purchase or sale agreements and related transaction documents
  • Explain contractual rights, obligations and important deadlines
  • Address legal issues identified during the pre-closing process
  • Communicate with the other professionals involved in the transaction when appropriate
  • Assist with the legal aspects of preparing for and completing the closing

Contact counsel early in the transaction

Real estate contracts often contain deadlines that begin running quickly. If you have already signed an agreement or received a proposed contract, provide it to the attorney as soon as possible together with any addenda, disclosures or correspondence related to the transaction.

What to expect when you call

Be prepared to explain whether you are buying or selling, the property involved, whether a contract has already been signed and any immediate concerns you have about the transaction. The attorney can then identify the documents that should be reviewed and the next deadlines that may require attention.

1Review the Transaction

Identify the property, parties, contract status and immediate deadlines.

2Address Legal Issues

Review documents and deal with contractual or closing concerns as they arise.

3Move Toward Closing

If retained, the firm provides legal guidance through the remaining transaction process.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I contact a real estate attorney?

It is helpful to involve counsel early, especially if a contract has already been signed or a deadline is approaching. Early review can help identify issues before they become more difficult to address.

What documents should I send to the attorney?

Provide the purchase or sale agreement, addenda, disclosures, inspection-related documents and any correspondence that may affect the transaction.

Can the firm help with inspection-related disputes?

The firm can review contract obligations and legal issues that arise during the inspection and pre-closing process and explain the available options.

What happens if a transaction is delayed or disputed?

The attorney can review the contract, identify the source of the dispute and explain the legal options available based on the facts and the agreement.
